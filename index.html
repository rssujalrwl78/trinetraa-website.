What is B2B SEO & AEO?

SEO helps your manufacturing business appear when buyers search on Google.

AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) helps structure your website content so search engines and AI-powered platforms can better understand your company, products, services and expertise.

For manufacturers, this means creating clear product information, structured content, FAQs, company information and credible online references that make your business easier to discover.

Questions

B2B digital marketing questions, answered.

What is B2B digital marketing for manufacturers?
B2B digital marketing for manufacturers is the use of digital channels such as Google Ads, Meta Ads, SEO, LinkedIn and content marketing to reach business buyers and generate qualified enquiries.
How can manufacturers generate B2B leads online?
Manufacturers can generate B2B leads through Google Ads, Meta Ads, SEO, LinkedIn marketing, product-focused content, landing pages and conversion tracking.
Is SEO useful for manufacturing companies?
Yes. SEO can help manufacturers reach buyers who are actively searching for specific products, suppliers, manufacturers and industrial solutions.
What is AEO for manufacturing companies?
AEO helps structure and optimise a manufacturer's online information so AI-powered search and answer systems can better understand and potentially reference the company when users ask relevant questions.
What does a digital marketing agency for manufacturers do?
A digital marketing agency for manufacturers plans and runs paid ads, search optimisation, LinkedIn and content marketing built around long B2B buying cycles, so industrial companies receive qualified enquiries instead of general traffic.
Which digital marketing channels work best for industrial companies?
It depends on the product and the buyer, but most industrial companies combine Google Ads for buyers who are already searching, Meta Ads and LinkedIn for reach among decision-makers, and SEO and AEO for long-term visibility.
Do you only work with manufacturing companies?
Mostly, yes. Industrial, B2B and export businesses are where I know the buying cycle well enough to be useful quickly. If you're outside that and the problem still fits, say so in the enquiry and I'll tell you honestly whether I'm the right person.
How much ad budget do I need to start?
For a single channel, ₹30,000–₹50,000 a month of media spend is usually enough to learn something real. Below that, the data comes in too slowly to optimise against. Spend goes directly to Google or Meta from your own account.
How long before I see enquiries?
Paid campaigns usually produce first enquiries in two to four weeks, and settle into a predictable cost per lead by month three. SEO and AEO are slower — expect four to six months for meaningful movement, which is why I rarely recommend running them alone.
Who owns the ad accounts and the creatives?
You do. Accounts are created under your business, I get access as a manager, and every creative file is handed over. If we stop working together you keep everything and nothing switches off.
